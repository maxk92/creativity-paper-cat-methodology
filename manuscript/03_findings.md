The number of judges "needed" for RQ1 will be read off the ICC-by-judge-count curve against this pre-specified threshold, not chosen post hoc. Candidate judge counts to be tabulated/plotted include 2, 3, 5, and 10 (achievable and consistent with the 10-rater benchmark panel).
