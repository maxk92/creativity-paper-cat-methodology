## (e) Word counts

I counted whitespace-separated tokens with a search pattern that treats each heading line, each embed line and each FILL IN marker as one token, located every section boundary by token position, and subtracted those single tokens together with list markers and the callout markup. Six positions were confirmed by direct probes and three paragraphs by hand counts. Consequences of the method:
- a citation key counts as one word (a rendered citation is longer, about 205 citation instances in the main text);
