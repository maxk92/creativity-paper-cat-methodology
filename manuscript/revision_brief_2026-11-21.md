A creative solution must be original (novel, rare, unusual) as well as functional (it must provide a solution to the given problem). Creativity in soccer describes the ability to choose solutions or execute actions in a given game situation that deviate from the norm, appear surprising, and are potentially advantageous for one's own team. It arises from the conscious perception of the situation and the recognition of possibilities that others do not see, or see only later. A creative action must be functional: it must (potentially) bring one's own team closer to the game objective (scoring, preventing goals). What matters is the intention or idea and the expected probability of success, not the actual outcome."
