# Submission sheet 4: Psychology of Sport and Exercise (PSE)

Publisher: Elsevier (official journal of FEPSAC). Portal: Elsevier Editorial Manager (the "Submit your article"
link on the PSE guide for authors). Guidelines checked 2026-10-27. Word (.docx) or LaTeX (.tex); **a PDF is not accepted as the source file**.

## A. Finish the content (same for every journal)
- [ ] Resolve all `[FILL IN …]` and `[AUTHOR TO CONFIRM …]` markers; remove the highlight filter, the `.mark` spans and the author-notes comment.
- [ ] Authors, affiliations, ORCIDs, corresponding author email (published with the article); get approval from all co-authors.
- [ ] Software versions (FI-6); re-render and check the reference list (any consistent style is accepted at submission).

## B. PSE-specific manuscript changes
- [ ] **Length: about 30 pages / 8,000 words including references, tables and figures.** The current main text is about
      6,400 words without references or tables, so it needs trimming. Move tables and figures to the supplement where possible.
- [ ] **Reframe for sport psychology**: the scope requires a "substantive theoretical or methodological contribution". Make clear
      in the introduction and discussion what the paper adds to measuring creativity in sport, and why sport-psychology readers should care.
- [ ] **Power / sample size**: PSE expects a-priori power estimates for quantitative studies. Add a paragraph explaining why
      a-priori power does not apply (exploratory secondary analysis of existing ratings, with no hypothesis tests planned), and report the precision achieved instead.
- [ ] Follow a recognised reporting guideline and say so (for example JARS-Quant).
- [ ] **Abstract: 250 words or fewer** (now 252), concise and self-contained; define abbreviations.
- [ ] **Keywords: 1–7**, single words where possible (avoid "and"/"of"). The current 7 are fine; consider shortening the multi-word ones.
- [ ] Address sex/gender (SAGER): report rater gender if it was recorded, or name its absence as a limitation.

## C. Required separate items
- [ ] **Highlights**: separate editable file with "highlights" in its name; 3–5 bullets of at most 85 characters each (spaces included).
- [ ] **Title page** (separate file): title, authors, affiliations, corresponding author, acknowledgments, funding.
- [ ] **Anonymised manuscript** (separate file): no names, affiliations, acknowledgments or identifying links (double-anonymised review).
- [ ] **Declaration of competing interests** (Elsevier tool, uploaded as .docx).
- [ ] Graphical abstract (optional).

## D. Statements in the manuscript
- [ ] **Ethics statement**: committee name, **date and reference number** of the approval, and informed consent of the raters.
- [ ] Funding sources and the sponsor's role (or "no involvement").
- [ ] **Declaration of generative AI and AI-assisted technologies in the manuscript preparation process**: a separate section before the references.
- [ ] Data statement: data sharing is encouraged (Option B). Give the repository link, or the reason for not sharing, at submission.
- [ ] CRediT author statement.
- [ ] Preregistration: state that the study was not preregistered (preregistration is optional at PSE).

## E. Files
- [ ] Word route: Quarto to .docx, single-column, no strikethrough or underline.
- [ ] LaTeX route: upload `.tex` and assets (the system builds the PDF).
- [ ] Figures as separate files at adequate resolution; tables editable.
- [ ] Supplementary material as a separate file with a caption for each item. It is published exactly as supplied.

## F. Cover letter
- [ ] Explain the fit with PSE and the methodological contribution to sport psychology. Give a short note on why the study is exploratory and has no a-priori power analysis.
- [ ] Confirm the work is original and not under review elsewhere; mention any preprint.

## G. In Editorial Manager
- [ ] Select the article type "Research paper"; enter metadata, authors and keywords.
- [ ] Upload the title page, anonymised manuscript, highlights, competing-interest declaration, figures, supplement and cover letter.
- [ ] Data statement step; check the built PDF; submit.
